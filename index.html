HR, Compliance & Workforce Services

1. Statutory & Labour Law Compliance

Protect your organization from legal risks, audits, and statutory penalties.

  • Labor Law Audits & Advisory: Thorough reviews of workplace practices, registers, and agreements.
  • Statutory Filings: End-to-end PF, ESI, PT, Minimum Wages & Shops/Establishments licensing.
  • POSH & Safety Governance: Policy structuring, committee formation & mandatory training.

2. End-to-End Payroll Management

Accurate, timely, and compliant payroll processing for seamless employee experience.

  • Payroll & Salary Structuring: Full-cycle execution, tax computation, attendance & payslip generation.
  • Tax Deductions & Year-End: TDS calculations, Form 16 issuance, quarterly returns & tax support.
  • Benefits Architecture: Gratuity structures, bonuses & competitive insurance programs.
Optimize payroll

3. HR Infrastructure & SOP Frameworks

Build structured, scalable internal processes for every stage of the employee lifecycle.

  • Hierarchy & JD Mapping: Competency matrices, career paths and reporting structures.
Build frameworks
